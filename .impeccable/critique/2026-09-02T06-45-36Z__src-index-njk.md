## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Not a stripped template, but not distinctive enough to pass the silhouette test yet. The page's spine — eyebrow label → serif H2 → paragraph → two buttons → stat strip → alternating paper/navy sections → 3-card grid → logo wall → blog teaser → navy closing CTA — is the exact rhythm of a Webflow/Framer "SaaS/consultancy" template. Two sections earn real distinctiveness: the sticky scroll-driven venture "record" reveal and the coverflow drag carousel. The other seven sections would silhouette identically against a hundred competitors wearing different brand colors.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs` against `src/pages/index.njk` + `src/_includes` returned exit code 2, 51 anti-patterns: `undersized-ui-text` (~30+, text below 11px — nav labels, chip tags, numerals, bylines), `kicker-above-heading` (7), `numbered-section-labels` (10), `em-dash-overuse` (19), `all-caps-body` (4), `wide-tracking` (3), `tiny-text` (3), `tight-leading` (1), `skipped-heading` (1, h1→h3 skips h2), `clipped-overflow-container` (1), `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` (3). No ignore rules were suppressing any of this.

Two categories the detector flagged (`kicker-above-heading`, `numbered-section-labels`) visually double as a *deliberate* editorial device (eyebrow labels, large translucent numeral watermarks) — possible false positives, but their sheer repetition (7 and 10 times respectively) is also exactly what Assessment A independently flagged as the page's biggest cognitive-load problem: one rhetorical shape repeated nine times running. The two assessments converge on the same underlying issue from different evidence.

**Visual overlays**: Browser injection succeeded (Playwright + the bundled `detect.js`, live-server on port 8400, cleanly stopped afterward). Full-page screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844 additionally surfaced something neither the detector nor a source-only read would catch: **large sections of the homepage rendered with zero visible content** in the stitched full-page capture — the world-map/globe section's content area, both sticky-media image columns in the Study/Work ecosystem panels, two full-bleed navy bands, the coverflow's card artwork, and a pre-footer section were all blank paper/navy with no text or imagery, on both desktop and mobile. This is either (a) a real bug where scroll/IntersectionObserver-gated or GSAP-driven content only paints on genuine incremental scroll and never fires for an automated full-page capture, or (b) sticky/pinned content that a stitched screenshot can't represent — the evidence doesn't distinguish the two, but CLAUDE.md's own documented rule ("`.fade-up`/`.grow-line` must render fully visible by default; JS only arms the hidden state") makes the first explanation plausible enough to warrant a direct look before assuming it's a screenshot artifact.

## Overall Impression

The page is well-intentioned and has real craft in two places (the sticky venture record, the cited-stat discipline), but "not premium enough" has three concrete, fixable causes rather than one vague taste problem: (1) a genuinely broken mobile CTA, (2) an unfinished radius sweep that reads as inattention rather than austerity, and (3) the same Prepare/Study/Work fact told four times running instead of building outward into scale/credibility/personalization — exactly the beats (10–30s "bigger than a consultancy," 30–60s "I can see myself using this") the brief's own emotional ladder says the page needs to hit and currently doesn't. The single biggest opportunity: stop restating the three-ventures fact a fourth time and spend that scroll budget on the "bigger than a consultancy" and "I can see myself using this" beats instead.

## What's Working

- **The sticky venture-record section** (`ecosystem-journey.njk`) is the one section that couldn't be lifted onto a generic template unchanged — pinned photo + progressively revealed record (number, stage, name, cited stat) is a real authored interaction, and it's the one place both the "no fake product UI" and "one bespoke interaction" project rules are honestly satisfied.
- **Stat attribution discipline** — every stat carries a real citation ("827,000 PTE Academic tests taken globally, 2022 — Pearson"). This is the "never invent business facts" non-negotiable working *for* the brand: it reads as more credible than competitors who assert stats with no source.
- **The academic network grid** — real logos, explicitly framed as "a curated sample, not a ranking," full list one click away. Honest, and still visually convincing at scale.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Mobile hero CTA is physically unusable.** At 390px, the ICEF trust badge overlaps and clips the "Explore our ventures" button, visibly cutting the label to "Explore our ver…" — confirmed independently by both assessments from separate screenshots. Why it matters: this is the page's primary action, broken on the most common viewport, not a taste call. Fix: stop absolutely-positioning the badge over the CTA row on mobile — stack it below the buttons, or move it lower on the page under `md:`. Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P0] Multiple sections render with zero visible content in a full-page capture, on both desktop and mobile.** The globe/map section's content area, both sticky-media image columns (Study/Work panels), two full-bleed navy bands, the coverflow's card art, and a pre-footer band all showed as blank in independent screenshots. Why it matters: if this reproduces for real visitors (not just automated capture) under any condition — slow scroll, no-JS, an observer that never fires — it directly contradicts the project's own documented rule that reveal-animated content must be visible by default, and a page that's silently missing half its imagery is the opposite of premium. This needs a direct look before anything else on this list, since it may be the actual root cause behind "doesn't feel premium." Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`

**[P0] The zero-radius sweep is unfinished, and the inconsistency reads as inattention, not austerity.** `rounded-full` survives on the eyebrow pill, badge-float's icon circle, and coverflow dots; `rounded-2xl`/`rounded-xl`/`rounded-lg`/`rounded-md` survive on badges, mobile nav, venture/expertise photos, and buttons — none of these are the documented single exception (999px nav pill). Meanwhile ecosystem-pathway panels, expertise cards, and the hero photo are hard 0px. Scrolling the page, corners visibly flip between sharp and rounded with no apparent rule. Why it matters: this is precisely the kind of half-finished detail that reads as "didn't finish," which is the user's own stated complaint. Fix: finish the sweep on every remaining category, or explicitly document which categories are exempt and why. Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] The same three-venture fact is told four times before the footer.** Preview cards ("What Admizz Is") → sticky record ("The Ecosystem") → pathway diagram ("From Potential to Opportunity") → coverflow ("Drag or scroll through the three"). Each is well made individually; stacked, they consume the exact scroll window the brief's ladder assigns to "10–30s: bigger than a consultancy" and "30–60s: I can see myself using this" on a fact the visitor already has by section 3. Fix: cut one (the coverflow and the pathway diagram cover nearly identical ground — keep whichever tests better) and use the recovered length for something not yet said: scale/geography, or a persona-routing moment ("I'm a student" / "I'm an institution" / "I'm an employer" — currently buried in one throwaway sentence at the very end). Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] ~30+ text nodes render below an 11px legibility floor, plus 19 em-dashes, 4 all-caps body passages, and one skipped heading level (h1→h3).** Chip tags (IELTS/PTE/SAT/GRE/GMAT), nav labels, numerals, and bylines are all sub-11px per the detector; independently corroborated as visually tiny in both screenshot passes. Why it matters: fine, dense micro-type reads as templated/technical rather than premium-editorial, and it's an accessibility floor violation, not a style preference. The skipped h2 is a real semantic/accessibility gap. Fix: raise the floor on chip/label text to at least 11–12px, reduce em-dash frequency in body copy, and insert the missing h2 level. Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`

## Minor Observations

- `text-navy-soft` eyebrow labels are a defined AA-safe token, not raw gold — the "gold never as text on light ground" rule is being correctly respected everywhere inspected.
- Gold is used twice in close proximity in the closing CTA band (fill on the button, italic text on "what's next.") — both technically compliant (navy background), but stacking two gold usages in one glance slightly dilutes gold's "seal" specialness.
- Header logo goes full-color-to-white via `brightness-0 invert` in the transparent navyHeader state, losing its own gold accent exactly when the page is trying hardest to look premium — confirm this is deliberate rather than a leftover from an earlier single-color logo asset.
- Academic network grid's flag emojis will render as two-letter codes on Windows Chrome/Android — SVG flag icons would be more reliable for a "premium global platform" positioning.
- Footer's "Our Ventures" column omits a link to `/insights/` — minor IA completeness gap.
- `clipped-overflow-container` (`div.ecosystem-story-media`) and the `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` findings (3 instances) are unconfirmed against pixels — worth a direct look, not dismissed as false positives.
- Founder/testimonial content mentioned in CLAUDE.md §4 wasn't visible in this homepage scroll — confirm it's meant to live on `/about/` only, per current IA.
