# 00 — The Master Brief (compressed)

The original brief ran ~47 sections. This is its load-bearing half: everything that should still
change a decision six months from now. **Read this for the ambition** — `01-AUDIT.md` has the facts
and `02-DIRECTION.md` has the plan, but neither carries the intent.

---

## 1. Mission

Rebuild **https://admizz.com/** as a complete digital experience transformation — not a redesign.

The site must **not** feel like:
- a traditional consultancy
- a typical corporate website
- a generic study-abroad website

It must feel like a **premium commercial digital product** that happens to belong to an education
and career company.

> **The target reaction: "This feels like the future of education and opportunity."**
>
> **Not: "Nice consultancy website."**

---

## 2. The role to occupy

Work as the combined creative director, product designer, UX strategist, brand designer, frontend
architect, motion designer, conversion strategist, accessibility engineer, performance engineer and
technical lead.

Do **not** behave like a developer converting a specification into components.

Optimise for *"How do I make Admizz feel like a completely different category of company?"* — never
for *"How quickly can I finish this?"*

---

## 3. The central design problem

| The current site says | The new site must say |
|---|---|
| "We are an education/workforce organization." | "We are an ecosystem that helps people discover and move toward their next opportunity." |

Users should feel: **ambition · possibility · movement · global opportunity · intelligence · trust ·
human guidance · technology · confidence · momentum.**

---

## 4. The user is the hero

Never make Admizz the only protagonist. Design around user motivations, not company capabilities.

Instead of *"We provide…" / "We offer…" / "We help…"*, design around:

- "I want to study abroad."
- "I don't know which country is right for me."
- "I need a better test score."
- "I want to find the right university."
- "I want to build my career."
- "I want to work internationally."

Admizz appears as the **guide, infrastructure and ecosystem** — not the hero.

> **Note:** this is an information-architecture decision, not a copy tweak. Doing it properly means
> the navigation is organised around user intent, not around Admizz's org chart. See
> `02-DIRECTION.md` §6.

---

## 5. Core experience model

The whole site should support this arc:

**DISCOVER** (what is possible?) → **EXPLORE** (where could I go?) → **UNDERSTAND** (what pathway
makes sense?) → **PREPARE** (what do I need?) → **ACT** (what should I do next?) → **GROW** (where
can this take me?)

---

## 6. Design philosophy

The site sits between: **premium technology company** × **global education platform** × **career
ecosystem** × **human advisory company**.

It should feel:

- Futuristic **but human**
- Premium **but approachable**
- Commercial **but trustworthy**
- Technological **but not gimmicky**
- Global **but personal**

And it must achieve **both** at once:
- **Commercial** — users want to explore, click, start.
- **Human** — users feel real people are behind it.

---

## 7. Every section must earn its place

Avoid the pattern of *heading → paragraph → three cards → button*, repeated ten times.

Build genuinely different experiences: destination discovery, pathway exploration, interactive
journeys, editorial storytelling, immersive imagery, animated maps, progressive timelines, large
typography, visual comparisons, scroll-driven storytelling, meaningful hover interactions.

**Design review standard — before any section is finished, ask:**

| Question | If yes → |
|---|---|
| Does this look like a template? | Redesign. |
| Does this feel like a consultancy? | Rethink. |
| Is this section visually predictable? | Find a stronger composition. |
| Is the animation decorative? | Remove it. |
| Is the copy too long? | Simplify. |
| Does the user know what to do next? | (If no) improve the interaction. |
| Does this feel premium? | (If no) refine. |

---

## 8. Hero

The first viewport must communicate opportunity, global scale, movement, education, career, human
guidance and modern technology.

Contains: strong headline · concise supporting statement · **one** primary CTA · one secondary CTA ·
immersive visual · subtle motion. **Avoid 5+ CTAs.**

A world map or 3D globe *may* be useful — but:

> **DO NOT ADD 3D JUST BECAUSE IT LOOKS COOL.** If a sophisticated 2D visual is stronger, use 2D.
> The visual must support the story.

Headline direction to explore: **"Your next move starts here."** — not locked; find something
stronger through research. The final line must feel ambitious, concise, memorable, global, human and
commercially strong.

---

## 9. CTA philosophy

Stop using "Contact Us" for everything. Use intent-based CTAs with a clear purpose:

| Intent | CTA |
|---|---|
| Primary | Start Your Journey |
| Exploration | Explore Your Options |
| Discovery | See Where You Could Go |
| Advisory | Talk to an Expert |
| Education | Explore Education |
| Institute | Prepare With Admizz |
| Workforce | Explore Careers |

> **Refinement from research:** **bound the commitment.** "Book a free *20-minute* call" outperforms
> an open-ended "Book Free Counselling", and costs nothing to change.

---

## 10. The ecosystem

Three business areas: **Admizz Education** (international education / study abroad) · **Admizz
Institute** (test preparation) · **Admizz Workforce Solutions** (skills, workforce, careers).

Do **not** show three cards. Make them feel connected: *one ecosystem, many ways forward.*

> ⚠️ **Reality check from the audit:** Admizz Institute has no website and no independent content,
> and Workforce Solutions is a US vocational-rehabilitation business with a genuinely different
> audience. The honest version of this section is in `02-DIRECTION.md` §3. Do not force a
> three-pillar story that the facts do not support.

---

## 11. Required section concepts

**"Where could you go?"** — an exploratory section around destinations, universities, pathways.
Interaction via map, destination cards, scroll movement, interactive routes. Sophisticated, not
gimmicky.

**Pathway** — a visually memorable journey that becomes a core brand metaphor:
DISCOVER (understand yourself) → PREPARE (build readiness) → APPLY (make the right choice) →
MOVE (take the next step) → GROW (build your future).

**Company story** — not a boring corporate timeline. Visual storytelling: 2015 THE BEGINNING /
2020 GOING GLOBAL / 2021 A NEW SCALE / TODAY BUILDING WHAT'S NEXT. **Verified facts only.**

**Founder** — not a generic "Meet our Founder" card. An editorial experience, e.g. *"A vision for
what's next."* **Never invent quotes.**

**Insights** — not `[Blog Card] [Blog Card] [Blog Card]`. A modern editorial publication.
Positioning: *"Ideas for what's next."*

---

## 12. Visual language

**Be:** bold · clean · editorial · modern · immersive · intelligent · premium · restrained.

**Avoid:** excessive glassmorphism · excessive gradients · glowing blobs · generic blue corporate
cards · AI-generated-looking graphics · excessive rounded rectangles · overuse of shadows · generic
stock photography.

**Colour:** do not blindly reproduce the current system. Inspect the existing brand, then *evolve*
it. A sophisticated neutral foundation with a distinctive brand accent is preferred.

**Typography:** must create identity. Explore contemporary typefaces — **do not automatically use
Inter.** Large display type, strong hierarchy, short body copy, editorial layouts, controlled line
lengths. Should feel *global + intelligent + contemporary.*

**Imagery:** authentic people, students, campuses, cities, travel, professional environments, real
moments. Every image must support the story.

---

## 13. ⛔ No "AI website" aesthetic — strict

Do **not** produce the generic generated-web look:

- dark navy background · purple/blue gradients · glowing blobs · glass cards
- floating 3D objects · excessive rounded corners · animated grid backgrounds · meaningless particles
- Tailwind defaults as brand colour (`blue-600` + `purple-500`)
- Inter everywhere with weights snapped to 400/500/700
- eyebrow pill with a sparkle emoji · uniform `rounded-2xl` · bento grids without hierarchy
- the fixed order: hero → logo cloud → 3 features → bento → testimonials → pricing → FAQ → CTA

> **The site should look *designed*, not *generated*.**

The full forensic tell-list, including the education-vertical specifics, is in `02-DIRECTION.md` §4.

Study its product-like storytelling, pacing, progressive disclosure, interaction, typography, visual
hierarchy, section transitions, editorial composition, whitespace.

>

## 15. Non-functional requirements

**Responsive** — mobile is not a compressed desktop. Design it intentionally. Test 320px+, mobile,
tablet, laptop, desktop, large desktop. Pay attention to typography, navigation, touch targets,
horizontal sections, animation, image cropping, CTA position.

**Performance** — premium does not mean slow. Optimised images, lazy loading, GPU-friendly
transforms, minimal JS, code splitting, efficient animation, proper font loading, Core Web Vitals.
No large libraries without meaningful value.

**Accessibility** — from the beginning, not retrofitted. Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible
focus, accessible buttons and navigation, sufficient contrast, proper heading hierarchy, meaningful
alt text, reduced-motion support, usable forms.

**SEO** — architecture for long-term organic growth around education, study abroad, international
education, test prep (IELTS, PTE, SAT, GRE, GMAT), careers, workforce solutions. **Never
keyword-stuff the interface.** SEO must stay natural.

## 16. Process

**Phase 0** Audit → **1** Experience architecture → **2** Creative direction → **3** Design system →
**4** Homepage → **5** Inner pages → **6** Quality pass.

Build the **homepage first** — it establishes the entire visual language. Then inner pages: About,
Our Story, Education, Institute, Workforce Solutions, Insights, Contact. Use the same design system,
but **do not make every page identical** — each needs its own narrative.

Before building large pages, establish the design system: typography scale, colour, spacing, and
reusable components (navbar, buttons, cards, section headers, container, grid, CTA, image blocks,
reveal animations, footer, editorial cards, venture cards, destination cards, journey components).
**Do not write one-off CSS for everything.**

**Technical:** inspect the existing stack; do not replace the framework without strong reason;
preserve useful infrastructure. Prefer reusable components, centralised tokens, predictable naming,
clean folder structure. Avoid giant page components, duplicated UI, inline magic values, unnecessary
abstractions and unnecessary dependencies.

---

## 17. Content authenticity — the rule that governs everything

> ## NEVER INVENT BUSINESS INFORMATION.

Do not invent: student numbers · university partnerships · visa success rates · application success
rates · revenue · awards · offices · countries · testimonials · reviews · partner logos · employee
counts · statistics · certifications.

If information is unavailable:
- create a flexible component, **or**
- use authentic general copy, **or**
- leave the section ready for real content, **or**
- recommend what real information should eventually be inserted.

**Authenticity is more important than marketing hype.**

> This is the constraint the audit turned into a strategy — see `02-DIRECTION.md` §7,
> *credibility without statistics*.

---

## 18. Final north star

> The site should feel like **a global education company, a modern technology platform and a premium
> career ecosystem built one digital experience together.**
>
> **FUTURISTIC · GLOBAL · HUMAN · COMMERCIAL · INTELLIGENT · PREMIUM · INTERACTIVE · CONFIDENT**

Build with intention. Build with restraint. Build with strong UX. Build with exceptional visual
hierarchy. Build with real content. Build for users. Build for scale.

**Make Admizz feel like the future of education and opportunity.**
